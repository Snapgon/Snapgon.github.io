It’s senior year of high school. So many things ask: who are you? What have you done? What do you want? I spent many an hour pondering these questions in silence. The emotions evoked from these reflections are melancholic and wistful to me. My series, NAME LATER, addresses these feelings through stark depictions of humanity. Set in bold black ink and large white space, the pieces set humans against the innumerable vastness of the world.
